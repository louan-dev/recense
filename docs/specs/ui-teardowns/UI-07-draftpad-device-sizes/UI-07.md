# UI-07 · Draftpad's editor box is sized for each device, not the Mac's box scaled

From: owner 2026-11-16 (WISHES.md: "per-device editor dimensions (iPad Air M2, iPhone 12 Pro Max,
portrait+landscape), not the Mac box scaled") · Status: `[~]`

## Problem
Every host gets the Mac's box: a 66ch column, 20px side padding, 28px top padding, and the font size the
host passes (`mobile/HostApp/iOS/Draftpad.swift`: 19 iPad, 17 iPhone). On a phone the column hits the screen
edges; in landscape it runs wide; the top padding takes room a short landscape screen can't spare.

## Rule
- One table, in `editor/src/devicelayout.js` (`deviceLayout(width, height, touch)`), picks the box from the
  viewport size in CSS px (= points under `width=device-width`) and orientation. Every host gets it, and so does
  the PWA on the same device.
- Touch devices only (`any-pointer: coarse`). The Mac (fine pointer) keeps today's box, unchanged.
- It is applied at load and again on every resize or rotation.
- The font size in the table is the default. A size the person picks later (`setFontSize`) wins until reload.
  The iOS host no longer passes a fixed size.
- Safe areas: side and bottom padding never fall below `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.

## The table (points; `ch` at the row's font)
| Device · orientation | Viewport | Font | Column max | Side pad | Top pad | Bottom pad |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone 12 Pro Max · portrait | 428×926 | 18 | 44ch (fills the width) | 16 | 20 | 96 |
| iPhone 12 Pro Max · landscape | 926×428 | 18 | 52ch | 24 (+ 47 safe area) | 8 | 48 |
| iPad 11-inch band (fallback, not a target) · portrait | 820×1180 | 20 | 58ch | 32 | 32 | 120 |
| iPad 11-inch band (fallback, not a target) · landscape | 1180×820 | 20 | 62ch | 48 | 24 | 96 |
| **iPad Air M2 13-inch (owner's iPad)** · portrait | 1024×1366 | 21 | 60ch | 40 | 36 | 120 |
| **iPad Air M2 13-inch (owner's iPad)** · landscape | 1366×1024 | 21 | 64ch | 56 | 28 | 96 |
| Mac / fine pointer (unchanged) | any | host's | 66ch | 20 | 28 | 96 |

How a viewport is matched (touch only):
- landscape = width > height.
- short side < 500 → phone row; short side < 900 → iPad 11-inch row; else → iPad 13-inch row.
- Other phones and iPads fall into the nearest row, so split view and other models still get a sane box.

Why these numbers (opinion, from the owner's rules "large text, narrow columns, minimal lateral movement"):
- 45–65 characters a line is the usual comfortable reading measure. Landscape stays under 65ch, so a
  wide screen centres the text instead of stretching it.
- iPhone landscape is only 428pt tall, so top padding drops to 8 and bottom to 48, keeping about 12 lines in view.
